Plan work

First out: the order with the earliest live LD or LC is planned first. Tick orders that still have shop work left (Not Yet Started, Ready for Steelwork, and in-progress jobs). The system books remaining work from that date and the shop status — Sam on profile cutting, John on tagging, Muruba (Mon / Wed / Fri) and Willard on welding, Thabile on plate cutting, Admire and Uriah on assembly. Grinding is auto-assigned to a person ticked for Grinding who has the most open time. You can change any name, or pick who grinds and tap Schedule grinding.

Clear planning

This deletes every booked slot, assignment, and other task on Planning, Journey, and People. Orders stay on the sheet. Type CLEAR to confirm.

Other task

Not an order — cleaning, a meeting, material, or any other job. It takes that person’s time and pushes shop work.
