Labour rates

R per hour per person. Time on an order uses that person's rate wherever they work — not the workstation (Welding, Cutting, and so on).

Steel rates

R per metre of the size / length logged on Profile Cutting, Plate Cutting, or Welding.

Backboard rates

R per m² of the size logged at the end of Assembly or Final QC. That rand is allocated onto the same order's material cost.
