Attach Outlook emails

Copy and Copy as link do not work from this Outlook into the browser. Drag the email instead.

  1. Open the enquiry → Save email from Outlook.
  2. In Outlook, click the email in the list on the left (the names, not the reading pane).
  3. Drag it onto Drop the Outlook email here until the subject appears.
  4. Click Save update.

If the drop does nothing, use Microsoft Edge (not Chrome). Open in Outlook then asks Windows to open Outlook — choose Open.
