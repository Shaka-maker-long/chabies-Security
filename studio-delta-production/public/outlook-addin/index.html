Attach Outlook emails

This Outlook does not show Add-ins or Copy as link. That is normal. Attach emails from the enquiry in Studio Delta.

  1. Open the enquiry in Studio Delta → Save email from Outlook.
  2. In Outlook, click the email once in Inbox or Sent.
  3. Drag it onto the box in Studio Delta, or right-click → Copy and paste into that box.
  4. Click Save update.

The email stays in Outlook. The app does not keep a .msg file. Open in Outlook launches Outlook desktop.
