Attach Outlook emails

The Home bar in Outlook will not show Studio Delta. That is normal on this PC. You attach emails from the enquiry in the office app, not from a missing Add-ins button.

Use this every time:

  1. In Outlook, click the email once in Inbox or Sent. Do not use a draft you are still writing.
  2. Right-click it and choose Copy as link. If that is missing, click the … on the far right of Home, or open the email and look on the Message tab.
  3. In Studio Delta, open the enquiry → Save email from Outlook → paste the link → Save update.

The email stays in Outlook. Open in Outlook on the enquiry launches Outlook desktop. Nothing is downloaded.

Only if your company allows add-ins: click File (top left of Outlook) → Get Add-ins or Manage Add-ins → My add-ins → Add a custom add-in → Add from URL, and paste:

Most office PCs hide that on purpose. Pasting the link is enough.
