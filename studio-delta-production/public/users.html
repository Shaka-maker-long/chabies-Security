Users

Production people only see the floor tasks you tick — not Orders, Debtors, or other office pages. Admin can open office pages. Untick See debtors if that admin must not see money still owing.

Tick Costing, Quoting, and Approval on the office people who usually do that work. New enquiries use those people automatically, so you do not pick them on every enquiry. You can still choose someone else on a single enquiry. Leave Approval unticked if cost sheets should skip approval.

The live database is SQLite on Railway. Google Sheets is not used. Every night at 02:00 Johannesburg the app copies SQLite, office JSON, and enquiry files into /app/data/backups (last 14 days stay on the volume). If Google Drive is set, that same copy is uploaded off the server and an email is sent. Backup now runs that copy immediately. Download SQLite is a copy for your PC.
