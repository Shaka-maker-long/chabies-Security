Studio Delta — features so far

BPMN of the live processes: enquiry to order, job card, shop clock (countdown, pauses, overtime), production, and completed work. This page is documentation only. It is not a screen in the app.

1. Enquiry to delivery

One order’s life. Office captures and quotes. Shop makes it. Client receives it.

2. Enquiry office

STATUS is not typed. Capture, costing, approval, quote, follow-up (7 days, max 3), then POP and create order. Closed: Not within scope, Not Interested, Rejected.

3. Job card and shop clock

Office generates the job card (sizes confirmed if not Standard). The floor starts a clock from Task times, ignores pauses, and records completed work with a trophy or overtime alarm.

4. Shop production

Main line plus plate cutting in parallel. Ready for Assembly can go to paint prep. Painting returns to Ready for Assembly.

5. Shift, pause, and overtime

Paid windows 07:45–12:00 and 12:30–15:45. Lunch and 16:00 auto-pause. After 16:00 and weekends need an overtime grant. Regular time cannot pass 8 hours without overtime.
